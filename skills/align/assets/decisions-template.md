# <Topic> - decisions

Each decision in a few lines: the title says what was decided, the text says
why. Numbered in the order they were made; the numbers never change. A decision
that needs a page is really several, or belongs in the PRD.

### 1. <What was decided, as a statement> - decided

Why, in one to three sentences.

### 2. <What was decided> - decided for now

Why. "Decided for now" means settled enough to build on, but expected to come
back.

Not chosen: the alternative someone will ask about, and why not, in one line.

## Open questions

Blocking: the answer changes the design or the first tasks, and the work does
not move on until it is answered. Parked: it affects one part only; the tasks
in "What waits" are marked `[!]` with the question number, and the rest goes
ahead. Questions for people outside the session are also in
`questions-for-<who>.md`.
